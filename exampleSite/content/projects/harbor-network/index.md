## The challenge

Fourteen harbor locations were using different dashboards, reporting rhythms and
handover practices. Teams could keep their local workflows, but the central
operations group could not reliably see the conditions that shaped each decision.

The project needed to improve shared visibility without replacing every local
tool at once.

## Delivery approach

The program was delivered in three phases:

1. Map the signals, decisions and handover points that mattered most.
2. Connect the existing field data into a shared operational layer.
3. Introduce a common control view and refine it with feedback from each site.

| Workstream | Delivered capability |
| --- | --- |
| Field visibility | Shared status from distributed locations |
| Control layer | Clear ownership, escalation and next actions |
| Handover | Consistent information for every shift |

## Outcomes

The harbor group gained a common operating picture while local teams kept the
tools and routines that worked for their sites. Escalations became easier to
understand, and the central team could compare conditions without waiting for a
manual report from each location.
