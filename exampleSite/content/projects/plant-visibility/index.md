## The challenge

Production, quality and maintenance teams were each working from a different
subset of the available data. A dashboard could show a deviation, but it did not
always explain whether the issue was isolated, recurring or already being handled
on the line.

The team needed a common context for action, not another collection of screens.

## Delivery approach

The program connected measurement signals, equipment status and operational notes
around the decisions that supervisors and technicians make every shift.

| Focus area | Delivery capability |
| --- | --- |
| Measurement | Consistent readings for process-critical conditions |
| Safety | Clear status and exception visibility |
| Handover | Shared context for the next shift |

The first release focused on three production lines. The data model and operating
patterns were designed so the same approach could expand without rebuilding the
system for every new line.

## Outcomes

Teams can now discuss production conditions using the same operational picture.
Supervisors see exceptions earlier, maintenance receives clearer context, and
handover conversations are grounded in current signals rather than separate
spreadsheets and dashboard screenshots.
