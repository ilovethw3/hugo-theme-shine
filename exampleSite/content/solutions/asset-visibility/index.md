## The economics of commercial battery storage

For most commercial and industrial electricity customers, demand charges — the fee based on your highest 15-or 30-minute demand reading each month — represent a significant and often poorly-understood portion of the electricity bill. A single demand spike on a hot afternoon can set the demand charge for the entire month.

A battery energy storage system addresses this directly. By monitoring your load in real time and discharging at the right moment, the NV-BESS 500 can shave 30–60% off your monthly demand peak. For a facility spending €50,000/year on demand charges, that is a €15,000–€30,000 annual saving from demand management alone.

## Stacking value streams

The most economically compelling C&I storage projects stack multiple value streams:

| Value stream | Typical annual saving |
|---|---|
| Demand charge reduction | €15,000 – €80,000 |
| Time-of-use arbitrage | €8,000 – €25,000 |
| Solar self-consumption uplift | €5,000 – €20,000 |
| Demand response revenue | €2,000 – €15,000 |

NV-EMS Cloud automatically manages all four value streams simultaneously, dispatching the battery to the highest-value opportunity at any given moment.

## Typical payback periods

Payback period varies by market, tariff structure, and system size, but most of our C&I projects achieve full payback within 4–7 years. With a system design life of 15 years and a 10-year capacity warranty, the economics over the full asset life are compelling.
