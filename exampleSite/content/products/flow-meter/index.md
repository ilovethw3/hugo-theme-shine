## Energy management that learns your site

NV-EMS Cloud connects to every NexVolt inverter and battery through a secure local gateway, pulling second-level telemetry into the cloud analytics engine. Within the first few weeks of operation, the AI dispatch algorithm learns the site's load patterns, solar generation profile, and grid tariff structure, and begins optimising dispatch schedules automatically.

## From alert to action in seconds

When a cell temperature deviation, communication loss, or state-of-charge anomaly is detected, NV-EMS Cloud generates an alert within seconds and routes it to the right person based on configurable escalation rules. The alert includes the affected asset, the severity, the recommended action, and a direct link to the relevant dashboard view — no manual log-diving required.

## Open by design

NV-EMS Cloud is designed to work alongside your existing tools, not replace them. Its REST API and webhook support allow energy data to flow into billing systems, building management platforms, carbon accounting tools, and custom dashboards. All data is owned by the site operator and can be exported at any time.
