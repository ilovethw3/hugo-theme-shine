## Built for the signal between the signals

Field Node X1 collects, normalizes and securely forwards operational data from
places where conventional infrastructure is difficult to maintain. Its
serviceable enclosure and low-power profile make it a strong fit for distributed
assets.

### Designed for practical deployment

The device supports flexible sensor inputs, remote diagnostics and staged
firmware updates. Teams can begin with one site and expand without changing the
operating model.
