## Connecting every energy source intelligently

The NV-Hybrid 100K manages three energy sources — solar PV, battery storage, and the utility grid — through a single, integrated power stage. Its triple-port architecture means there is no energy conversion penalty when power flows from solar directly to the battery or directly to load. This translates into measurable efficiency gains over multi-inverter approaches.

## Millisecond-level grid response

Grid frequency and voltage deviations are detected and acted upon in under 20 ms. When the grid fails, the NV-Hybrid 100K transitions to island mode instantaneously, maintaining continuous power to the loads it protects. When the grid recovers, reconnection is synchronised automatically, with no intervention required.

## Built for outdoor installation

IP65-rated and rated for operation from -25°C to +60°C, the NV-Hybrid 100K is designed for outdoor cabinet or shelter installation in climates from northern Europe to the Middle East and Southeast Asia. The fan-assisted thermal management system is field-serviceable, and all major components are accessible from the front panel.
