description: "Clear answers about our products, projects, delivery process and support."
faq:
  - question: "How do I choose the right product for my application?"
    answer: |
      Start with the operating environment, required performance, interfaces and installation constraints. Our team can review your requirements and recommend a suitable product configuration.

      If you already have technical drawings or a specification sheet, you can include those details in your enquiry.
  - question: "Can products be configured for a specific project?"
    answer: |
      Yes. Product options, interfaces, mounting arrangements and documentation can be adapted when the project requirements call for it. The available options depend on the product family and the intended operating conditions.
  - question: "Do you support system integration?"
    answer: |
      We support integration planning, interface definition, commissioning coordination and operational handover. The scope is agreed during the project discovery stage so responsibilities and deliverables are clear.
  - question: "How long does a typical project take?"
    answer: |
      Timing depends on the scope, approval process, technical complexity and delivery requirements. After reviewing the brief, we provide a practical schedule with key decisions, dependencies and milestones.
  - question: "What information should I include in a quotation request?"
    answer: |
      Please include the application, operating conditions, required quantity, target delivery date, installation environment and any relevant standards or drawings. The more context you provide, the more useful our initial response can be.
  - question: "Do you provide documentation and training?"
    answer: |
      Documentation, commissioning guidance and operational handover can be included in the project scope. Training and additional support are available when they are needed by the customer’s team.
  - question: "What support is available after delivery?"
    answer: |
      We can provide troubleshooting guidance, maintenance information, replacement recommendations and follow-up engineering support. The available service level depends on the product and the agreed support arrangement.
  - question: "How can I contact your team?"
    answer: |
      Use the contact form, email or telephone details on the Contact page. Include the product, project or application you are asking about so that your enquiry can be routed to the right person.
