faq:
  - question: "What battery chemistry does NexVolt use?"
    answer: "All NexVolt BESS products use lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cells sourced from Grade-A manufacturers. LFP chemistry offers superior thermal stability, a longer cycle life (≥ 6,000 cycles), and a safer operating profile compared to NMC — making it the preferred choice for commercial and industrial applications."

  - question: "What is the warranty on the NV-BESS 500?"
    answer: "The NV-BESS 500 carries a 10-year product warranty with a guaranteed capacity retention of ≥ 80% at end of warranty period. Our NV-EMS Cloud platform monitors each system remotely throughout the warranty term, so issues are typically identified and resolved before they affect performance."

  - question: "What certifications does NexVolt hold?"
    answer: "NexVolt products are certified to IEC 62619 (electrochemical storage safety), UL 9540 (North American energy storage system safety), ISO 9001:2015 (quality management), CE (EU market access), and UN 38.3 (lithium battery transport safety). Additional grid-connection certifications are available for specific markets on request."

  - question: "Can I retrofit storage to an existing solar installation?"
    answer: "Yes. The NV-Hybrid 100K inverter AC-couples to most existing grid-tied solar installations without replacing your existing solar inverter. For new installations, DC-coupling via the NV-Hybrid 100K is also supported and delivers slightly higher round-trip efficiency. Our engineering team will assess your site and recommend the optimal integration approach."

  - question: "How long does installation typically take?"
    answer: "A single NV-BESS 500 cabinet can typically be installed and commissioned within one working day, assuming civil groundwork and grid-connection permits are already in place. Larger multi-cabinet or multi-megawatt projects are planned individually — contact us for a project timeline estimate."

  - question: "What remote monitoring and support is included?"
    answer: "Every NexVolt system includes a 12-month subscription to NV-EMS Cloud, our cloud-based energy management platform, which provides 24/7 real-time monitoring, automated alerts, and performance reporting. After the first year, NV-EMS Cloud is available as an annual subscription. On-site support and preventive maintenance are available through our global partner network."

  - question: "How do I get a quote or start a project?"
    answer: "Use the contact form on our Contact page and include your approximate load data (peak demand in kW, daily consumption in kWh) and project location. Our solutions team will respond within two business days with a preliminary sizing recommendation and indicative pricing."
